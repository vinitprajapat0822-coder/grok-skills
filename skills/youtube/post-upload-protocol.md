# YouTube Post-Upload Protocol

**Category**: YouTube Growth / Retention Protection  
**Source**: Video v-0zv2L9N0I (transcript)  
**Purpose**: Protect early signals (retention, audience matching, CTR testing) in the critical first 48 hours.

## Never Do (9 Mistakes)

1. **Watch your own video right after upload**  
   You become one of the first viewers. Short watch = kills early retention data.

2. **Upload directly as Public**  
   Processing not finished → low quality for first viewers → bad first impression.

3. **Share link with friends/family immediately**  
   Weak retention + wrong audience signals (news/cooking watchers confuse algorithm about your niche).

4. **Change title / thumbnail / chapters too quickly**  
   Resets YouTube’s testing. Data collected so far is discarded.

5. **Buy views or subscribers**  
   External random traffic confuses audience targeting + violates TOS + no Browse/Suggested push.

6. **Check analytics every 10 minutes**  
   Early data is noisy and incomplete → leads to panic decisions (especially title changes).

7. **Delete the video if views are low**  
   Videos can explode weeks/months later. Treat every video as a lottery ticket. Give at least 30 days.

8. **Manually delete every negative comment**  
   Time waste. Top negative comments still poison first impression for new viewers.

9. **Disappear after uploading**  
   Zero engagement in first hours = weak signal to algorithm.

## Do Instead

- Quality-check **before** upload (offline).
- Upload as **Unlisted** → set title/desc/tags/end screens → wait for HD processing → then Public.
- Wait 24–48 hours before sharing outside niche audience.
- Use **A/B testing** (Studio) for titles/thumbnails instead of mid-test changes.
- Focus only on **CTR + Average View Duration** after 2–3 days.
- Set blocked words once (Community Moderation).
- Reply to comments in first hour + pin a question or discussion starter.
- Post a Community Post at the exact same time as publish for double notification.

## When to Apply
Every single upload. Especially critical for small channels and new videos.
