# Early Engagement Protocol (First Hour)

**Category**: YouTube Algorithm Signals  
**Source**: Video v-0zv2L9N0I  
**Why**: First hours of engagement (comments + replies) are strong positive signals.

## Rules
1. After making the video Public, stay active for the first 60 minutes.
2. Reply to every early comment (even short ones).
3. Pin one of these:
   - A genuine question related to the video
   - A slightly controversial / discussion-starting statement
4. Goal: start a conversation in the comments so the comment section looks alive.

## After First Hour
- Close Studio.
- Do not refresh analytics every few minutes.
- Re-open after 2–3 days and look only at CTR + Average View Duration.
