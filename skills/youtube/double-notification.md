# Double Notification Trick

**Category**: YouTube Growth / Notifications  
**Source**: Video v-0zv2L9N0I  
**Goal**: Get two notifications for the same video so more subscribers actually open it.

## Steps
1. Publish the video (Public).
2. **Immediately** create a Community Post that contains:
   - Video title
   - Video link
   - Thumbnail (or screenshot)
3. Subscribers already receive the normal “new video” notification.
4. They also receive a separate Community Post notification for the same video.

## Why it works
Many people ignore or miss the regular video notification. The Community Post acts as a second chance / reminder.

## Notes
- Works better when you already post occasionally on Community tab.
- Even with low subscribers, starting the habit early is good.
- Do not spam Community tab; keep it valuable.
