# 9 Things You Should Never Do After Uploading a YouTube Video

**Source**: https://youtu.be/v-0zv2L9N0I  
**Transcript**: https://youtubetotranscript.com/transcript?v=v-0zv2L9N0I  
**Extracted**: 2026-10-31

1. Watching your own video immediately after upload (hurts early retention).
2. Making the video Public before processing finishes.
3. Sharing the link with friends & family right away (bad retention + wrong audience signals).
4. Changing title / thumbnail / chapters too early (resets testing).
5. Buying views or subscribers.
6. Checking analytics every 10 minutes.
7. Deleting the video if it has low views in the first week.
8. Manually deleting every negative comment one by one.
9. Disappearing after upload (no engagement signal).

**Bonus Trick**: Publish video + simultaneous Community Post → double notifications to subscribers.
